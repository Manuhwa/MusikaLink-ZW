Good practice for informal traders & farmers

Short educational tips for Zimbabwe farm-gate and market sales. Use alongside MusikaLink listings, ward hubs, and the Prices & Safety till check.

  1. 1

    Keep a simple sale record

    Note date, product, grade, quantity, unit price, total, and buyer name/phone. A notebook or SMS to yourself counts.

  2. 2

    Use agreed weights and grades

    Agree the scale and Grade A/B/C (or local equivalent) before loading. Disputes drop when both sides see the same measure.

  3. 3

    Prefer recognised markets / ward hubs

    Sell at known markets or ward aggregation points when you can — clearer prices, witnesses, and safer payment trails.

  4. 4

    Keep proof of sale

    Keep the SMS receipt, till confirmation, or a signed note. Helps with follow-ups and shows you traded in good faith.

Simple sale record (template)

Copy into a notebook or phone note for each sale:

  • Date: _______________
  • Product: _______________
  • Grade (A/B/C): _______________
  • Quantity + unit: _______________
  • Unit price & total: _______________
  • Buyer name / phone / till: _______________
